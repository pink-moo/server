How To Play
Collect resources around the map to build a village. Your Windmills generate gold over time. But make sure to protect them from other players.
Controls

Gameplay:

Movement: W A S D / Arrow Keys
Aim: Move Mouse
Lock Rotation: X
Attack: Left Click
Break: Right Click
Toggle Auto Gather: E
Select Item: 1-9 or Click
Spam Food: Q

UI:

Add Map Marker: C
Chat: Enter
Quick Open Command: Slash Close Windows: Esc
Toggle Client Menu: Tab

Hacks:

Place Trap: F
Place Spike: V
Place Turrets: H
Place Walls: B
Place Boostpad + 2 Spikes: Y
Place 4 Spikes: J
Place 4 Turrets: K
Range Combo: G
Toggle Insta: R
Toggle 1-tick: T
Toggle Spin: P
Grind Weapons: N
Reset State (Debug): Dot

Commands:

/mod - Say mod version
/ping [times]|stop - Say ping
/bunker - Place 4 mines
/pardon <player-sid> - Remove player from blacklist
/echo [all|stop] - Echoes nearest player's chat
/follow - Follow nearest player
/set predict|spin [value] - Set values (debug)
